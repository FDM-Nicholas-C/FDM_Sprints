# Persona: Complaint Analyst
## Role and responsibilities:
- Verify the summary of the AI Assistant

- Receive and solve claims

- Decide whether or not to escalate

- Decide whether or not to accept recommended decision

- Decide whether or not to do a complaint claim manually
## Information required
- Customer emails

- AI Summary

- AI recommendations

- Supervisor Contact information
## Permissions
- Customer emails

- Supervisor Contact information

- Can view company policies

- Can view customer history
## Constraints
- Can’t handle escalated claims

- Can’t view audit logs

# Persona: Supervisor
## Role and responsibilities:  Handle escalated claims
## Goals: Successfully handle escalated claims from customers
## Tasks: Receive the escalated claim, solve the claim.
## Information required:
- The AI summary

- The Analyst's decision 

- Audit log of the claim
## Permissions:
- Can view Audit logs

- Can view Complaint Analysts

- Can view complaint emails

- Can view company policy

- Can handle escalated claims

- Time constraint
