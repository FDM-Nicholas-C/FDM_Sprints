## Known limitations

- Dates must be `YYYY-MM-DD` (optionally with `00:00:00`); other formats are rejected as invalid.
- Status matching is exact and case-sensitive (`Closed`); variants like `closed` are not normalised.
- Rows are excluded rather than repaired, so counts only cover rows passing validation.
- Input is CSV; the `.xlsx` extract was converted to `complaints.csv` first.
