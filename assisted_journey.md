- Customers send complaints.

- Complaints are analyzed by the AI in the investigation phase

- AI correctly picks the policy around the issue in the evidence phase

- AI uses given tools to query the customers information from db in the investigation phase

- AI summarizes it, producing the recommendation and triaging the urgency, if not enough information is found, the Complaint Analyst takes over

- AI suggests a decision for the claim, and responsibility is handed off to the Complaint Analyst to approve or deny that decision

- Human can escalate if AI gives a high enough urgency, and responsibility is handed off to the Supervisor

- The supervisor then handles the claim.
